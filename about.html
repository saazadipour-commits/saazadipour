01 — CORPORATE OVERVIEW

A Global Perspective In Medical Technology.

Azadipour is an Oman-registered healthcare and medical technology company focused on international business development and the advancement of advanced medical technologies.

We currently hold dedicated sales lines for select global robotic products and are in active negotiations with several leading robotics companies to further expand our portfolio.

Through our strategic hub in Oman, we connect healthcare organizations and international manufacturers. We are continuously upgrading our relationships with premier international hospitals to integrate next-generation surgical technologies and comprehensive hospital solutions into the region.
