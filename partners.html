01 / OUR PARTNERSHIP APPROACH

Connecting Technology
with Healthcare Markets.

Azadipour aims to develop professional business relationships with international manufacturers, medical technology developers and healthcare organizations.

Our focus is on identifying suitable technologies, understanding local market requirements and facilitating communication between suppliers and potential healthcare customers.

Cooperation opportunities are evaluated individually, with consideration for market conditions, product suitability, regulatory requirements and mutually agreed commercial terms.
