INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

Building connections. Creating opportunities.

Azadipour works to connect international medical technology manufacturers and healthcare solution providers with regional market opportunities across Oman and the wider GCC.

WHO WE WORK WITH

Connecting expertise across healthcare.

We welcome discussions with organizations developing technologies and solutions that respond to the evolving requirements of hospitals and healthcare providers.

01 / MANUFACTURERS

Medical Technology Manufacturers

Companies developing advanced medical devices, surgical systems, diagnostic technologies and hospital equipment for regional and international markets.

02 / TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPERS

03 / SOLUTION PROVIDERS

OUR PARTNERSHIP APPROACH

A structured approach to market development.

Every market and technology requires a clear understanding of its commercial, operational and regulatory context. Azadipour aims to build cooperation around transparent communication, realistic opportunities and clearly defined responsibilities.

Potential cooperation models may be discussed according to the product portfolio, target market, project status and mutual business objectives.

01 Initial introduction and company profile exchange
02 Product portfolio and market suitability review
03 Identification of relevant market opportunities
04 Discussion of commercial cooperation models
05 Definition of project scope and next steps
