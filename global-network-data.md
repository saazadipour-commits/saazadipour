## Design principles

- Worldwide geographic perspective.
- Accurate country and continent geometry.
- Monochrome platinum and silver visual identity.
- Static geographic projection.
- No user-controlled globe rotation.
- No fabricated hospital locations.
- No unverified international office claims.
- No implication of confirmed partnerships through illustrative map routes.

## Technical dependencies

- d3-geo 3
- topojson-client 3
- world-atlas 2

Dependencies are loaded from jsDelivr CDN.

## Maintenance

The map is illustrative and represents international connectivity,
not confirmed customer locations, distribution agreements,
hospital projects or physical company offices.
