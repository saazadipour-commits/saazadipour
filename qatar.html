COMPLIANCE

Quality
before volume.

Medical products entering the Qatari market must be assessed according to the applicable regulatory, registration, import and procurement requirements.

Product documentation, certifications, technical specifications, supplier qualification and appropriate local representation may be required depending on the product category and route to market.

Every opportunity should therefore be evaluated commercially and from a regulatory perspective before supply is initiated.
